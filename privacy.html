Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Channel Uploader is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads videos to YouTube channels that belong to the owner. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for permission to upload videos to the chosen YouTube channel (and, for channel setup, to manage that channel's own settings and playlists). It uses YouTube API Services to do this.

What it stores

The sign-in token Google returns is saved only on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any server other than Google's. The tool does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it has no analytics or advertising.

Google and YouTube

Use of YouTube data is also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the owner can also delete the saved token files.

Contact

Questions: use the contact address shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
